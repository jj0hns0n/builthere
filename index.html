What AI can actually do for a small business

Not a chatbot on your website. Real work:

  • Answer questions from your own data. Your books, your operations system, your spreadsheets, connected and queryable in plain English. "Show me every customer who bought last spring but hasn't this year."
  • Do the retyping. Invoices entered, orders keyed between systems, reports assembled. The routine transfer work that eats an afternoon, done by software while your people do the job you actually hired them for.
  • Work every inbox at once. Shared mailboxes and personal ones, connected so one assistant can search across all of them. "Find every quote request from last month that nobody answered." No forwarding rules, no logging in one at a time, no wondering where it landed.
  • Draft the replies that repeat. Quote requests, order status, proposals, follow-ups. It reads the thread, pulls the numbers off your price list and your order history, and leaves a reply waiting in drafts in your voice. You read it and hit send.
  • Catch what leaks. Reconciling across systems finds the shipment that was never invoiced, the commission that was never collected, the stock count that can't be right. In a small business, those leaks are real dollars.
  • Watch the numbers every month. Control reports that run themselves: what changed, what doesn't add up, what needs a decision. On your desk without anyone spending a day compiling it.

One rule that never bends: AI drafts words; code does arithmetic. Any number you or your customers will rely on (a price, a payout, a reconciled balance) is computed by deterministic, auditable software. A language model never invents a dollar figure.
